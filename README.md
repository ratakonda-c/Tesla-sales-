Tesla-Sales-Analytics-Dashboard
This project presents an interactive Tesla Sales Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze and visualize vehicle sales performance across multiple business dimensions. The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders understand key sales trends, customer behavior, and revenue distribution in a clear and data-driven manner.

The dashboard highlights essential Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) including Units Sold (75.9K), Total Revenue ($4,177M), and Average Revenue per Vehicle ($55K). These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of overall business performance and enable high-level monitoring of sales growth.

To understand customer demographics, the dashboard includes gender-wise sales distribution and sales mode analysis, covering online, in-store, and third-party channels. This helps identify dominant purchasing behaviors and preferred sales channels. Additionally, age-group analysis reveals which customer segments contribute most to Tesla’s sales, supporting targeted marketing and customer engagement strategies.

The dashboard also provides a state-wise sales breakdown, highlighting regional demand across major states such as California, Texas, New York, and Washington. This regional insight helps identify high-performing markets and potential expansion opportunities.

A detailed model-wise and color-wise sales table offers insights into customer preferences across Tesla models including Model 3, Model Y, Model S, Model X, and Cybertruck. The analysis shows how different color options contribute to total sales, enabling better inventory and production planning.

To track performance over time, a year-wise revenue trend visual illustrates revenue growth from 2018 to 2024, making it easier to identify peak performance years and market fluctuations.

🎯 Business Value

This dashboard enables data-driven decision-making by providing actionable insights into sales performance, customer behavior, regional trends, and product demand. It is ideal for use by sales managers, business analysts, and executives seeking a comprehensive view of Tesla’s sales analytics.
